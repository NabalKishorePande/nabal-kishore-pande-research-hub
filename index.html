Research

Nabal Kishore Pande is an independent researcher working across decision systems, scholarship preparation intelligence, decision intelligence, execution intelligence, research methodology, evidence-based decision making, and research frameworks.

This Research Hub provides a consolidated research record covering research domains, projects, publications, datasets, frameworks, external research profiles, persistent identifiers, research methods, evidence, and provenance.

The page separates substantive research content from external distribution and identifier records. Research content is documented within the research record, while external profiles and persistent identifiers are maintained as separate evidence layers.

Provenance principle:

Research objects should be understood through their relationships. A research question can lead to a project; a project can produce evidence and analysis; analysis can produce a paper, report, dataset, or framework; and a published research object can have a persistent identifier and an external record.

This page documents those relationships as a structured research record rather than treating individual outputs as isolated items.
