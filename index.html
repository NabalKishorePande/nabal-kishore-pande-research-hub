Overview

From research to usable publication

This publishing approach connects research, structured knowledge, practical resources and digital public access.

A publication does not need an ISBN to provide research value, practical value or public access. The important question is whether the underlying work is useful, structured, documented and accessible.

This page therefore treats digital publication as part of a wider research and knowledge system rather than as an isolated publishing format.

Research

Evidence comes first

Publications begin with research, observation, analysis and the organisation of relevant evidence.

The purpose is not simply to collect information. Research is developed into a form that can support understanding, comparison, planning or decision-making.

Where a claim matters, the underlying evidence and source should remain identifiable.

Framework

Structure turns research into a system

Research can be organised into frameworks, methods, models, sequences and decision structures where structure improves practical use.

A framework gives the reader a defined way to examine a problem, compare evidence or carry out a task.

Knowledge becomes actionable

Research and frameworks can be converted into guides, tools, checklists, workflows, assessment resources and other practical outputs.

These resources help readers use the underlying research instead of only reading about it.

Digital Publication

Publication is an access layer

Digital publishing provides a practical way to present the resulting knowledge in a structured, readable and accessible form.

The publication can support direct reading, reference, reuse and continued development without depending on a traditional book-production model.

Publication Model

One system, different outputs

The publication process connects research with reader-facing outputs without treating every publication as the same type of product.

  • Research Evidence, analysis, source material and organised knowledge.
  • Framework A structured method for understanding, evaluating or applying the research.
  • Guide / Tool A practical resource derived from the research and framework.
  • Digital Publication A readable, accessible and publicly available knowledge output.
  • Distribution Placement across appropriate research, publishing, archive and professional platforms.
  • Access Readers can find, read, apply, reference and revisit the resulting work.
