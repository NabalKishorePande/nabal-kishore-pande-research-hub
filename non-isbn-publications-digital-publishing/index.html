Non-ISBN Publications & Digital Publishing

Research-based knowledge converted into structured, usable and accessible digital publications.

From research to usable publication

This page presents a publishing approach centred on research, structured knowledge and practical digital outputs.

The work includes publications that do not depend on an ISBN to provide value, access or practical use. The focus is on making knowledge available in forms that readers can understand, apply and revisit.

Research

Evidence comes first

Publications begin with research, observation, analysis and the organisation of relevant evidence.

The purpose is not simply to publish information. The research is developed into a form that can support understanding, comparison, planning or decision-making.

Framework

Structure turns research into a usable system

Research is organised into frameworks, methods, models and clear sequences where structure improves practical use.

This allows a reader to move from information to a defined way of examining a problem or carrying out a task.

Guide / Tool

Knowledge becomes actionable

Where appropriate, the research and framework are converted into guides, tools, checklists, workflows or other practical resources.

These outputs are designed to help readers apply the underlying research rather than only read about it.

Digital Publication

Publication is an access layer

Digital publishing provides a practical way to present the resulting knowledge in a structured and accessible form.

The publication format can support direct reading, reference, reuse and continued development without making the publication dependent on a traditional book-production model.

Publication model

The publishing process connects research with a reader-facing output.

  • Research Evidence, analysis and organised knowledge.
  • Framework A structured method for understanding or applying the research.
  • Guide / Tool A practical resource derived from the underlying framework.
  • Digital Publication A readable and accessible public-facing output.
  • Access Knowledge made available for readers to use, reference and revisit.

Access and use

Non-ISBN publication does not mean informal publication. The value of a digital publication depends on the quality of its research, structure, clarity, documentation and accessibility.

The objective is to create durable digital knowledge resources that can be read independently and used as practical references.
